    // 01 — the idea

    What makes BTX different

    • 21,000,000

      Fixed supply

      At most 21 million BTX, as with Bitcoin.

      More on fixed supply →

    • A × B

      Mining by matrix multiplication

      Instead of hashing, miners multiply large matrices, the work AI accelerators are built for.

      More on mining by matrix multiplication →

    • PQ

      Post-quantum signatures

      Coins are locked with signatures meant to withstand quantum computers: ML-DSA-44 (NIST FIPS 204) for everyday spending, SLH-DSA (FIPS 205) as a hash-based fallback.

      More on post-quantum signatures →

    • Knots

      Built on Bitcoin Knots

      The node derives from Bitcoin Knots v29.2, a variant of Bitcoin Core.

      More on built on bitcoin knots →

    // 03 — read this once

    Before you buy or receive BTX

    7 checks

    Where people buy, with its warning →

    1. 01

      Make a backup before money arrives. In btxd, backupwalletbundlearchive writes one encrypted file; keep it and its passphrase offline and apart. Any backup can spend your coins: plain exports at once, the archive once opened. Restore it once to test it.

    2. 02

      Check the receiving address in full, not just its start and end. BTX addresses start with btx1z.

    3. 03

      Send a small test amount first. Wait for at least 10 confirmations before you treat a payment as final; short reorganisations of the chain happen several times a day.

    4. 04

      Before you use an exchange or an OTC seller, check that withdrawals really work. A price on a website is not a market you can sell into.

    5. 05

      Other coins also trade as BTX. A market is only for this chain if its deposit addresses start with btx1z.

    6. 06

      A "model" or "reference" price is an estimate, not an offer that anyone has to honour.

    7. 07

      Nobody legitimate asks for your seed. Messages offering help, "recovery" or guaranteed returns are scams.
