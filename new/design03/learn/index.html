About BTX

What BTX is, and what the next release brings.

What is BTX?

01

Fixed supply

At most 21 million BTX, as with Bitcoin. A block comes about every 90 seconds; its reward of 20 BTX halves every 525,000 blocks, roughly every 18 months.

Source: Fixed supply ↗

02

Mining by matrix multiplication

Instead of hashing, miners multiply large matrices, the work AI accelerators are built for. Since block 185,000 a node that checks blocks itself replays each one exactly on a qualified NVIDIA (CUDA) or Apple (Metal) GPU; nodes without one follow signed checks from others. There is no CPU mining.

Source: Mining by matrix multiplication ↗

03

Post-quantum signatures

Coins are locked with signatures meant to withstand quantum computers: ML-DSA-44 (NIST FIPS 204) for everyday spending, SLH-DSA (FIPS 205) as a hash-based fallback. These P2MR addresses start with btx1z.

Source: Post-quantum signatures ↗

04

Built on Bitcoin Knots

The node derives from Bitcoin Knots v29.2, a variant of Bitcoin Core. btxd, btx-cli, the RPC interface and PSBTs work the way Bitcoin users know them; what differs is the proof-of-work, the signatures and some network rules.

Source: Built on Bitcoin Knots ↗

Roadmap and papers

What this community has written down. Every one of them lives in the repository, so each change is visible as a commit and anyone can correct a figure.

  • Roadmap ↗ What the community can do by itself and what depends on the core team, in two columns, with a place to put your name against a lane.Open for edits
  • The bootstrap window and the 1,000,000 BTX deployment reward ↗ The first question most newcomers ask, answered from chain data: what the first 50,000 blocks produced, under which consensus rules, and what public data can and cannot show.Draft - under review
  • Network fund ↗ How the network could pay for its own infrastructure: contributions, multisig custody, published rules and payouts against verified uptime.Discussion paper
  • Venue structure and the licensing boundary ↗ What a decentralised exchange can be on a chain without a virtual machine, where the regulatory line runs, and the models that remain open. Not legal advice.Draft for discussion
  • How to change something here ↗ What you can edit freely, what needs a second pair of eyes, and how to report a wrong figure if git is not your thing.
  • Review and test programme - one pull request per bug class ↗ Test coverage measured on swapnet-v2 at a5d1637f (now part of the release line), the recurring bug classes behind 317 fix pull requests since August, one sweep per class that closes it with a rule, and checks before a final release. A proposal by contributors; the maintainer decides what lands.Measured 12 November 2026 at commit c6f9678d; coverage 14 November at a5d1637f
