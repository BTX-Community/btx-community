    New to BTX

    BTX is a post-quantum fork of Bitcoin with a fixed maximum supply and its own proof-of-work (MatMul).

    All 7 steps for Running a node
    1. Easiest start at home: easynode ↗Installs and supervises btxd for you.
    2. On a server ↗v0.34.16 has two Linux x86_64 archives for glibc 2.38 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13): a plain one, and -cuda13 for GPU nodes (sm_120: RTX 50 and RTX PRO Blackwell cards; driver not included). No official Windows or macOS archive.
    3. Before you install: the checklist →Where to download, how to check the file, and the settings for a node with coins.
    4. Start from a signed snapshot ↗Catches up in minutes. A sync from genesis currently stops at block 126,978 (issue #272).
    5. Node stuck at a height? ↗The node help explains each known case and its fix.
    6. Have a spare machine? →Seven ways to give BTX infrastructure, from a node to a fuzz box.
    7. Run a signer ↗Signers (GPU attestors) need a GPU that passes btxd's self-test and must stay online. The official CUDA archive covers Blackwell cards (sm_120); other NVIDIA cards need a build from source and may not qualify. Follow the developers' operator runbook.

    Before you buy or receive BTX

    Before you install a node release →
    1. 01Make a backup before money arrives. In btxd, backupwalletbundlearchive writes one encrypted file; keep it and its passphrase offline and apart. Any backup can spend your coins: plain exports at once, the archive once opened. Restore it once to test it.
    2. 02Check the receiving address in full, not just its start and end. BTX addresses start with btx1z.
    3. 03Send a small test amount first. Wait for at least 10 confirmations before you treat a payment as final; short reorganisations of the chain happen several times a day.
    4. 04Before you use an exchange or an OTC seller, check that withdrawals really work. A price on a website is not a market you can sell into.
    5. 05Other coins also trade as BTX. A market is only for this chain if its deposit addresses start with btx1z.
    6. 06A "model" or "reference" price is an estimate, not an offer that anyone has to honour.
    7. 07Nobody legitimate asks for your seed. Messages offering help, "recovery" or guaranteed returns are scams.
